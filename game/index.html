LANTERN LANES

A three-lane card battler. Light your lanterns, outplay the Keeper.

How to play

  1. Play creatures into lanes. Mana grows by 1 every turn, to 10.
  2. Creatures attack down their lane. Clear a lane to strike the enemy hero.
  3. Kindred: playing a creature gives your other creatures of its faction +1 attack.
  4. Win by reducing the rival hero from 20 life to 0.
Ember ≈ Tide Moss

Mouse or keyboard — Enter ends your turn, Esc cancels a selection.
